Menu organization

The sidebar tab creation order is unchanged. Each tab now has an explicit section order applied after its controls and saved settings are constructed. Controls keep their instances, IDs, callbacks, and saved values.

Player starts with movement and flight, followed by camera, follow/animation tools, barriers, and performance. Movement toggles are paired with their sliders. Blobman separates seating and flight settings. Grab keeps throw/modes and held-object details before a combined line appearance section. Objects & Toys starts with toy management and building before shapes and appearance. Visuals places player/world ESP first, then camera, environment, and cosmetics. World & Travel starts with travel and puts servers and audio below world tools. KeyBinds starts with menu/emergency shortcuts; notification and appearance sections are also ordered explicitly.

Repeated headings are consolidated, empty headings omitted, and future unlisted sections retained at the end. Search uses the resulting section names. The organizer changes presentation only.

Validation: Luau compilation, 23 existing feature checks, and an organizer runtime check covering section merges, stable ordering, control preservation, search metadata, future sections, and repeated organization. No live Roblox visual check was available.
