The IDs follow the R6 Animate dance sets reproduced in [Roblox Emote IDs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-emote-ids/1161079) and the [R6/R15 animation comparison](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/r6-to-r15-animation-list-from-roblox/4079574). These are three classic dance families with three variants each, not nine unrelated dance styles.

New presets require R6 and play as looped Action-priority tracks through the existing Animator. R15 is rejected for these presets without altering legacy entries. See Roblox's [animation playback documentation](https://create.roblox.com/docs/animation/using).

Both production distribution files match. The bundle compiles and 132 tests pass, covering the catalog, rig gate, speed, selection changes, respawn and cleanup. Live asset availability depends on the experience and Roblox permissions.
